What a listing means

A candidate is unreviewed. A reviewed entry includes domain-control and protocol-test evidence for its stated protocol and revision or reference. This does not certify safety, complete conformance or independent interoperability.

The directory is optional. A listing never grants access, changes a configured endpoint, or replaces Agent Login Core domain discovery. An AgentID listing does not claim Core compatibility. Expired evidence requires a new review.
